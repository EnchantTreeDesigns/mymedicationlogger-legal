# MyMedicationLogger — Support

Need help? Email **support@enchanttreedesigns.com** and we'll get back to you.

## Common questions

**How do I import my medications?**
Settings → Import medications. Pick "From MyChart PDF," "From FollowMyHealth PDF," or "Paste text" depending on your source. The app extracts your medication list and lets you confirm before saving.

**My calendar reminders aren't showing up.**
Make sure MyMedicationLogger has Calendar permission: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars → MyMedLogger → enabled. Also confirm a default calendar is set in the iOS Calendar app.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
iOS Settings → tap your name at top → Subscriptions → MyMedicationLogger Pro → Cancel.

**How do I restore a previous purchase?**
Open the app → Settings → Privacy & Data → Upgrade to Pro → tap "Restore purchase" at the bottom of the paywall.

**Is my data backed up?**
Not automatically. MyMedicationLogger is local-only and doesn't use cloud sync. Use Settings → Export to back up your data manually as a PDF or Excel file.

**Where is my data stored?**
On your device only, in iOS's private app sandbox. Nothing is sent to a server. See the [Privacy Notice](privacy.html) for details.

## Bug reports & feature requests

Email **support@enchanttreedesigns.com** with as much detail as possible. Screenshots and the iOS version you're on help a lot.
